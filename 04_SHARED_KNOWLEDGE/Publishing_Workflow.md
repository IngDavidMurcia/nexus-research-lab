# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the official publication workflow adopted by the NEXUS Research Lab.

Its objective is to define the standardized lifecycle governing the preparation, review, approval, publication and long-term preservation of scientific outputs generated within the laboratory.

This workflow SHALL ensure:

- scientific quality;
- editorial consistency;
- methodological compliance;
- ethical integrity;
- publication readiness;
- long-term knowledge preservation.

---

# 2. Scope

This workflow applies to every scientific deliverable produced within NEXUS, including:

- journal manuscripts;
- conference papers;
- technical reports;
- systematic reviews;
- software documentation;
- white papers;
- research datasets;
- supplementary materials.

The workflow SHALL remain independent of publisher or journal.

---

# 3. Publishing Principles

Every publication SHALL preserve the following principles:

- scientific integrity;
- methodological rigor;
- editorial quality;
- transparency;
- traceability;
- reproducibility;
- accountability;
- continuous quality improvement.

Publication SHALL represent the final stage of scientific validation rather than merely document completion.

---

# 4. Publication Lifecycle

Every publication SHOULD conceptually follow the lifecycle below.

```text
Research Planning

↓

Research Execution

↓

Evidence Collection

↓

Scientific Writing

↓

Technical Review

↓

Scientific Review

↓

Integrity Audit

↓

Publication Preparation

↓

Final Approval

↓

Submission

↓

Publication

↓

Knowledge Preservation
```

Each stage SHALL generate documented outputs supporting full traceability.

---

# 5. Publication Stages

The publication workflow SHALL be divided into sequential stages.

Stage 1

Research Planning

Objectives:

- define publication goals;
- identify target audience;
- determine publication strategy.

Deliverables:

- Project Object
- Research Plan

---

Stage 2

Research Execution

Objectives:

- execute approved methodology;
- collect evidence;
- document activities.

Deliverables:

- Experimental records
- Research logs
- Evidence repository

---

Stage 3

Scientific Writing

Objectives:

- prepare manuscript;
- organize results;
- document methodology.

Deliverables:

- Draft manuscript
- Figures
- Tables
- References

---

---

Stage 4

Technical Review

Objectives:

- verify technical consistency;
- evaluate methodological implementation;
- confirm documentation completeness.

Responsible Role:

Research Architect

Primary Deliverables:

- Technical Review Report
- Methodology Verification
- Architecture Assessment

Exit Criteria:

- Methodological consistency verified.
- Technical deficiencies resolved.
- Documentation complete.

---

Stage 5

Scientific Review

Objectives:

- evaluate scientific validity;
- assess evidence quality;
- review logical consistency;
- verify conclusions.

Responsible Role:

Scientific Reviewer

Primary Deliverables:

- Scientific Review Report
- Review Comments
- Revision Requests

Exit Criteria:

- Scientific quality approved.
- Evidence adequately supports conclusions.
- Reviewer observations resolved.

---

Stage 6

Research Integrity Audit

Objectives:

- verify ethical compliance;
- validate citation integrity;
- inspect reproducibility;
- evaluate governance compliance.

Responsible Role:

Research Integrity Auditor

Primary Deliverables:

- Integrity Audit Report
- Compliance Assessment
- Risk Register

Exit Criteria:

- Ethical compliance verified.
- Citation verification completed.
- Integrity risks accepted or mitigated.

---

Stage 7

Publication Preparation

Objectives:

- prepare final publication package;
- apply editorial standards;
- finalize references;
- verify formatting.

Responsible Role:

Publication Specialist

Primary Deliverables:

- Publication Package
- Submission Files
- Cover Letter
- Supplementary Material

Exit Criteria:

- Publication package complete.
- Editorial standards satisfied.
- Target journal requirements fulfilled.

---

Stage 8

Final Approval

Objectives:

- authorize publication;
- verify readiness;
- approve official release.

Responsible Role:

NEXUS Director

Primary Deliverables:

- Publication Approval
- Final Decision Record

Exit Criteria:

- Scientific approval granted.
- Governance approval completed.
- Publication authorized.

---

# 6. Publication Governance

Publication governance SHALL ensure that every deliverable satisfies institutional quality requirements before external dissemination.

Governance SHALL preserve:

- scientific quality;
- methodological consistency;
- ethical responsibility;
- editorial conformity;
- evidence traceability;
- institutional accountability.

---

## 6.1 Decision Authority

Publication authority SHALL follow the hierarchy below.

```text
Research Architect

↓

Scientific Writer

↓

Scientific Reviewer

↓

Research Integrity Auditor

↓

Publication Specialist

↓

NEXUS Director
```

Each participant SHALL approve only the activities within their assigned responsibilities.

---

## 6.2 Publication Gates

The workflow SHALL define mandatory approval gates.

Gate 1

Research Complete

↓

Gate 2

Technical Review Approved

↓

Gate 3

Scientific Review Approved

↓

Gate 4

Integrity Audit Approved

↓

Gate 5

Publication Package Approved

↓

Gate 6

Director Authorization

Projects SHALL NOT bypass mandatory gates.

---

# 7. Publication Quality Assurance

Every publication SHALL undergo systematic quality assurance before external dissemination.

Quality assurance SHALL evaluate:

- scientific validity;
- methodological consistency;
- editorial compliance;
- ethical compliance;
- citation integrity;
- reproducibility;
- publication readiness.

Quality assurance SHALL be documented for every publication.

---

## 7.1 Quality Verification

Quality verification SHOULD include:

- technical verification;
- scientific peer review;
- editorial review;
- citation validation;
- integrity assessment;
- formatting verification.

Each verification activity SHALL produce traceable records.

---

## 7.2 Publication Readiness

A publication SHALL be considered ready for submission when:

- scientific objectives have been achieved;
- methodology has been validated;
- evidence adequately supports conclusions;
- references have been verified;
- editorial standards have been satisfied;
- integrity audit has been completed;
- institutional approval has been granted.

Submission SHALL NOT occur before all readiness criteria are satisfied.

---

## 7.3 Post-Publication Activities

Following publication, projects SHOULD preserve:

- published version;
- supplementary materials;
- datasets where applicable;
- software artifacts;
- review history;
- publication metadata.

Knowledge preservation SHALL support future reproducibility and institutional memory.

---

# 8. Compliance

A publication workflow SHALL be considered compliant if it:

- follows the defined publication lifecycle;
- satisfies mandatory publication gates;
- preserves scientific integrity;
- maintains methodological traceability;
- applies editorial standards;
- verifies references;
- complies with ethical requirements;
- documents governance decisions.

Non-compliant publications SHOULD return to the appropriate workflow stage for correction.
