# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the ethical principles governing all scientific activities performed within the NEXUS Research Lab.

These principles define the institutional expectations regarding responsible research conduct, scientific integrity, transparency and ethical decision-making throughout the research lifecycle.

Every research project SHALL comply with these principles unless superseded by stricter legal or institutional requirements.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to every research activity conducted within the NEXUS Research Lab, including:

- scientific investigations;
- engineering research;
- computational research;
- software development;
- AI-assisted research;
- literature reviews;
- systematic reviews;
- publication activities;
- research dissemination.

These principles SHALL apply independently of discipline, execution platform or publication venue.

---

# 3. Ethical Principles

Every research activity SHALL adhere to the following ethical principles:

- honesty;
- integrity;
- objectivity;
- transparency;
- accountability;
- fairness;
- responsibility;
- respect for intellectual property;
- respect for human knowledge;
- scientific independence.

Scientific objectives SHALL NEVER justify unethical practices.

---

## 3.1 Scientific Responsibility

Researchers SHALL:

- pursue truthful scientific outcomes;
- acknowledge uncertainty;
- report limitations;
- communicate findings honestly;
- preserve methodological transparency.

Scientific credibility SHALL take precedence over publication success.

---

## 3.2 Professional Conduct

Every participant SHALL demonstrate professional conduct by:

- respecting collaborators;
- recognizing intellectual contributions;
- avoiding conflicts of interest whenever possible;
- maintaining scientific neutrality;
- documenting significant decisions.

Professional conduct SHALL remain consistent throughout the research lifecycle.

---

# 4. Responsible Research Conduct

Research activities SHALL be conducted in a responsible, transparent and reproducible manner.

Responsible conduct includes:

- accurate documentation;
- transparent methodology;
- proper citation;
- evidence preservation;
- responsible data management;
- objective reporting;
- reproducible procedures.

Responsible conduct SHALL extend to every stage of the project.

---

## 4.1 Intellectual Honesty

Researchers SHALL NOT:

- fabricate evidence;
- falsify results;
- selectively omit contradictory evidence;
- misrepresent scientific findings;
- intentionally mislead readers.

Any identified violation SHALL be documented and escalated according to governance procedures.

---

## 4.2 Conflict of Interest

Potential conflicts of interest SHOULD be disclosed whenever they may influence scientific interpretation.

Conflict disclosure MAY include:

- financial interests;
- institutional interests;
- personal relationships;
- commercial partnerships;
- funding influence.

Undisclosed conflicts MAY compromise research credibility.

---

# 5. Scientific Integrity

Scientific integrity SHALL be preserved throughout the complete research lifecycle.

Integrity includes:

- methodological consistency;
- citation integrity;
- documentation integrity;
- version integrity;
- evidence integrity;
- publication integrity.

Integrity SHALL be continuously monitored rather than verified only at project completion.

---

# 6. Ethical Compliance

Every research project SHALL demonstrate compliance with applicable ethical principles throughout its lifecycle.

Ethical compliance SHALL include:

- adherence to institutional policies;
- compliance with applicable regulations;
- documentation of ethical decisions;
- evidence of responsible research practices;
- transparency in scientific communication.

Ethical compliance SHALL be continuously evaluated during project execution.

---

## 6.1 Human-Centered Research

Research involving human participants SHALL:

- respect human dignity;
- preserve participant autonomy;
- protect privacy and confidentiality;
- minimize foreseeable risks;
- comply with applicable ethical approval requirements.

When human subjects are involved, all required approvals SHALL be obtained before research activities begin.

---

## 6.2 Data Ethics

Research data SHALL be managed responsibly.

Data management SHOULD ensure:

- lawful acquisition;
- appropriate access control;
- privacy protection;
- secure storage;
- responsible sharing;
- proper retention policies.

Sensitive data SHALL receive additional protection proportional to its risk.

---

# 7. AI Ethical Considerations

Artificial Intelligence SHALL be employed as a research support tool while preserving human scientific responsibility.

Researchers SHALL remain accountable for:

- scientific conclusions;
- methodological decisions;
- interpretation of evidence;
- publication content;
- ethical compliance.

AI systems SHALL NOT replace scientific accountability.

---

## 7.1 Responsible AI Usage

AI-assisted research SHOULD:

- disclose significant AI assistance where appropriate;
- verify AI-generated content;
- validate references independently;
- identify potential hallucinations;
- preserve human oversight.

Human review SHALL remain mandatory before publication.

---

## 7.2 Transparency

Projects utilizing AI SHOULD document:

- AI systems employed;
- scope of AI assistance;
- validation procedures;
- human review activities;
- identified limitations.

Transparency SHALL strengthen trust rather than replace scientific verification.

---

# 8. Compliance

A research project SHALL be considered ethically compliant if it:

- follows institutional ethical principles;
- demonstrates responsible research conduct;
- preserves scientific integrity;
- protects intellectual property;
- applies responsible AI practices;
- maintains ethical documentation;
- complies with governance requirements.

Projects failing ethical compliance SHOULD be suspended until deficiencies are addressed.
