# Instructions

This template SHALL be used to prepare the official scientific report for every completed research project conducted within the NEXUS Research Lab.

The report SHALL document the complete scientific process, ensuring methodological transparency, evidence traceability and publication readiness.

Additional sections MAY be incorporated when required by the target publication venue.

# 6. Methodology

Describe the complete methodological approach.

Include:

- research design;
- experimental setup;
- study population;
- materials;
- instruments;
- procedures;
- data collection;
- analytical methods.

Methodology SHALL remain reproducible.

---

# 7. Results

Present the research findings objectively.

Results SHOULD be organized using:

- tables;
- figures;
- charts;
- experimental observations;
- quantitative measurements;
- qualitative findings.

Interpretation SHOULD be limited in this section.

---

# 8. Discussion

Interpret the reported findings.

Discuss:

- significance of results;
- comparison with previous studies;
- implications;
- limitations;
- unexpected observations;
- methodological considerations.

Every major interpretation SHOULD be supported by evidence.

---

# 9. Conclusions

Summarize the principal conclusions.

Conclusions SHALL:

- answer the research questions;
- address the stated objectives;
- remain supported by the reported evidence;
- avoid unsupported claims.

---

# 10. Recommendations

Provide recommendations for:

- future research;
- methodological improvements;
- technological developments;
- practical applications.

Recommendations SHOULD remain consistent with the reported findings.

---

# 11. Limitations

Describe limitations affecting the research.

Examples include:

- sample size;
- experimental constraints;
- computational limitations;
- data availability;
- methodological assumptions;
- external validity.

Limitations SHALL be explicitly acknowledged.

---

# 12. Ethical Compliance

Summarize ethical aspects including:

- ethics approvals;
- informed consent (if applicable);
- privacy considerations;
- AI usage disclosure;
- conflict of interest declarations.

This section SHALL comply with Ethics.md and AI_Guidelines.md.

---

# 13. References

List every cited source.

References SHALL comply with:

- Citation_Standards.md
- Editorial_Standards.md

Persistent identifiers SHOULD be included whenever available.

# 14. Appendices

Optional appendices MAY include:

- raw data summaries;
- experimental protocols;
- software documentation;

- mathematical derivations;
- questionnaires;
- laboratory records.

Appendices SHOULD remain traceable.
