# Instructions

This template SHALL be used to conduct the official integrity audit of every completed research project before publication.

The audit SHALL independently verify ethical compliance, methodological traceability, citation integrity, reproducibility and governance conformity.

The audit SHALL remain objective, evidence-based and fully traceable.

# Template Usage Notes

This template documents the official institutional integrity audit of a research project.

The audit SHALL evaluate the integrity of the research process rather than the scientific merit of the results.

Scientific quality SHALL remain the responsibility of the Scientific Reviewer, while editorial preparation SHALL remain the responsibility of the Publication Specialist.
