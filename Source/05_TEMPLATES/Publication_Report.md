# Instructions

This template SHALL be used to evaluate the publication readiness of a completed research project.

Its purpose is to verify that every scientific, editorial and administrative requirement has been satisfied before submission to an external publication venue.

This report SHALL document the complete publication preparation process.

# Template Usage Notes

This template documents the editorial preparation of a scientific work.

Its objective is to certify that the manuscript satisfies all institutional and publication-specific requirements before submission.

This report SHALL NOT reassess scientific validity; scientific evaluation is the responsibility of the Scientific Reviewer, while integrity verification belongs to the Research Integrity Auditor.
