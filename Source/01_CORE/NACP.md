# NEXUS Agent Communication Protocol

Version 1.0

---

# 1. Purpose

The NEXUS Agent Communication Protocol (NACP) defines the normative communication contract governing every interaction between components of the NEXUS Research Lab.

Its objective is to ensure that all agents exchange information in a structured, deterministic, traceable and interoperable manner.

Every component of the laboratory SHALL implement this protocol.

No proprietary communication mechanism may replace NACP without maintaining protocol compatibility.

---

# 2. Scope

NACP applies to:

• NEXUS Director

• Research Architect

• Scientific Writer

• Scientific Reviewer

• Publication Specialist

• Research Integrity Auditor

• Future Specialist Agents

• Workflow Engine

• Project Object

• External Integrations

---

# 3. Design Principles

The protocol has been designed according to the following principles.

## 3.1 Standardization

Every message SHALL follow the same structural format regardless of the sender.

---

## 3.2 Interoperability

The protocol SHALL remain independent of any AI provider or orchestration framework.

Compatible implementations include:

- Google Opal
- Vertex AI Agent Engine
- OpenAI Agents SDK
- CrewAI
- LangGraph
- AutoGen
- Semantic Kernel

---

## 3.3 Traceability

Every communication SHALL be uniquely identifiable and reconstructable.

No message shall exist without provenance.

---

## 3.4 Extensibility

Future protocol versions SHALL allow new blocks without breaking existing implementations.

---

## 3.5 Determinism

Given identical inputs, protocol interpretation SHALL produce equivalent semantic meaning across implementations.

---

## 3.6 Human Readability

Although machine-oriented, NACP SHALL remain readable by researchers, reviewers and developers.

---

---

# 5. Communication Model

NACP adopts a **hub-and-spoke communication model**.

```
                    User
                      │
                      ▼
              NEXUS Director
                      │
     ┌────────────────┼────────────────┐
     ▼                ▼                ▼

Research Architect

Scientific Writer

Scientific Reviewer

Publication Specialist

Integrity Auditor
```

Key rules:

- Specialists SHALL NOT communicate directly with each other.
- All interactions SHALL pass through the Director.
- The Director SHALL preserve the canonical Project Object.
- Each specialist SHALL receive an immutable snapshot of the approved project state.

# 6. Message Structure

## 6.1 Purpose

Every communication exchanged within the NEXUS Research Lab SHALL be represented as a NACP Message.

A message is the smallest atomic communication unit of the laboratory.

Every message SHALL be self-descriptive.

A message SHALL contain all information required for interpretation without requiring implicit context.

---

## 6.2 Canonical Structure

Every NACP Message SHALL contain six mandatory sections.

```
Header

↓

Body

↓

Quality

↓

Routing

↓

Metadata

↓

Validation
```

Each section serves an independent architectural purpose.

---

## 6.3 Complete Message Model

```yaml
NACP_Message:

    Header:

    Body:

    Quality:

    Routing:

    Metadata:

    Validation:
```

No section may be omitted.

---

## 6.4 Design Goals

The message structure has been designed to guarantee:

• Determinism

• Explainability

• Traceability

• Auditability

• Platform Independence

• Forward Compatibility

---

Each message SHALL belong to exactly one Message Type.

Expected Improvement

# 10. Quality Block

## 10.1 Purpose

The Quality Block represents the self-assessment and quality evaluation associated with a NACP Message.

It provides quantitative and qualitative indicators describing the confidence, completeness, rigor and reliability of the generated output.

Every specialist SHALL include a Quality Block.

---

## 10.2 Canonical Structure

```yaml
Quality:

    Confidence:

    Completeness:

    Scientific_Rigor:

    Editorial_Quality:

    Integrity:

    Explainability:

    SQI_Contribution:

    Overall_Score:
```

---

## 10.3 Confidence

Represents the specialist's confidence in the generated conclusions.

Scale:

0–100

Interpretation:

| Score | Interpretation |
|--------|----------------|
| 90–100 | Very High |
| 75–89 | High |
| 60–74 | Moderate |
| Below 60 | Low |

---

## 10.4 Completeness

Evaluates whether the assigned task has been fully completed.

Possible values:

- Complete
- Partial
- Insufficient

---

## 10.5 Scientific Rigor

Measures methodological robustness.

Applicable to:

- Architect
- Reviewer
- Integrity Auditor

---

## 10.6 Editorial Quality

Measures language and publication readiness.

Applicable primarily to:

- Scientific Writer
- Publication Specialist

---

## 10.7 Integrity

Represents the perceived scientific integrity of the evaluated content.

This metric SHALL NOT replace the dedicated Integrity Audit.

---

## 10.8 Explainability

Measures how well recommendations are justified.

Opaque reasoning SHALL reduce this score.

---

## 10.9 SQI Contribution

Represents the estimated contribution of this execution to the global Scientific Quality Index.

---

## 10.10 Overall Score

Represents the weighted quality evaluation of the current message.

---

Each error SHALL specify:

```yaml
Recovery:

    Error_Code:

    Severity:

    Description:

    Recommended_Action:

    Responsible_Component:

    Retry_Allowed:
```

---

## 14.5 Escalation Rules

Critical errors SHALL immediately return control to the Director.

The Director SHALL determine whether:

- retry execution;
- assign another specialist;
- rollback the workflow;
- terminate the project.

---

## 14.6 Failure Logging

Every failure SHALL be appended to the Project History.

Failure records SHALL NEVER be deleted.

# 15. Compatibility

## 15.1 Purpose

Compatibility ensures that different implementations of the NEXUS Agent Communication Protocol (NACP) can exchange messages without loss of semantic meaning.

Protocol compatibility SHALL be maintained across platforms, AI providers and workflow engines.

---

## 15.2 Platform Independence

NACP SHALL remain independent from any specific implementation technology.

Reference implementations MAY include:

- Google Opal
- Google Vertex AI Agent Engine
- Google Gems
- OpenAI Agents SDK
- ChatGPT Custom GPTs
- CrewAI
- LangGraph
- Microsoft Semantic Kernel
- AutoGen
- Future orchestration platforms

Protocol semantics SHALL remain identical.

---

## 15.3 Backward Compatibility

Minor protocol versions SHALL remain backward compatible.

Receiving systems SHALL ignore unknown optional fields while preserving mandatory sections.

---

## 15.4 Forward Compatibility

Future protocol versions MAY introduce:

- new message blocks;
- new metadata fields;
- additional routing options;
- extended quality metrics.

Previously defined mandatory fields SHALL preserve their semantic meaning.

---

## 15.5 Unsupported Features

Implementations SHALL explicitly declare unsupported protocol capabilities.

Example:

```yaml
Compatibility:

    NACP: 1.0

    Supports:

        Quality Block

        Routing Block

        Metadata Block

    Does_Not_Support:

        Parallel Execution

        Streaming Messages
```

---

# 16. Versioning

## 16.1 Purpose

Versioning guarantees controlled protocol evolution.

NACP adopts Semantic Versioning.

```
MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH
```

---

## 16.2 Major Version

Increment when:

- breaking protocol changes;
- incompatible message structures;
- mandatory field modifications.

---

## 16.3 Minor Version

Increment when:

- optional fields are added;
- protocol capabilities are extended;
- new message types are introduced.

---

## 16.4 Patch Version

Increment when:

- documentation corrections;
- editorial improvements;
- clarification of existing rules.

---

## 16.5 Version Declaration

Every message SHALL declare the protocol version.

Example

```yaml
Header:

    Protocol: NACP

    Protocol_Version: 1.0.0
```

---

## 16.6 Deprecation Policy

Deprecated fields SHALL remain supported for at least one MAJOR protocol version unless explicitly stated otherwise.

---

# 17. Security

## 17.1 Purpose

The protocol SHALL preserve message authenticity, integrity and confidentiality.

---

## 17.2 Confidentiality

Sensitive project information SHALL be protected throughout the workflow.

Sensitive information includes:

- unpublished manuscripts;
- confidential datasets;
- proprietary methodologies;
- institutional documentation;
- intellectual property.

---

## 17.3 Integrity

Messages SHALL preserve their original meaning after transmission.

Unauthorized modification SHALL invalidate the message.

---

## 17.4 Authenticity

Every message SHALL identify:

- originating agent;
- execution timestamp;
- protocol version;
- project version.

---

## 17.5 Traceability

Every message SHALL remain traceable through:

Message_ID

Correlation_ID

Project_ID

Workflow_ID

Agent_ID

Timestamp

---

## 17.6 Non-Repudiation

Generated reports SHALL become immutable once accepted by the Director.

Historical reports SHALL remain permanently accessible.

---

## 19.1 Purpose

The protocol has been intentionally designed for long-term evolution.

Future extensions SHALL preserve compatibility whenever possible.

---

## 19.2 Possible Extensions

- streaming communication;
- distributed execution;

- event-driven workflows;
- semantic routing;

- support Body specification;
- implement Quality Block;
- implement Routing Block;
- implement Metadata Block;
- implement Validation Rules;
- implement Error Handling;
- preserve traceability;
- preserve protocol semantics.

| Level | Description |
|--------|-------------|

| L1 | Structurally Compatible |
| L2 | Operationally Compatible |
| L3 | Fully NACP Compatible |

---

## 20.4 Certification Criteria

A Reference Implementation SHALL satisfy:

- 100% mandatory requirements.
- Complete interoperability.
- Complete traceability.
- Full protocol validation.
- Semantic compatibility.
- Platform independence.

---

## 20.5 Conformance Statement

Any implementation claiming NACP compatibility SHALL conform to the requirements defined in this specification.

Extensions MAY be implemented.

Mandatory protocol behavior SHALL NOT be altered.
